A2Z Electronics

Electronics Contract Manufacturing Company in USA

A2Z Electronics LLC is a US-based electronics contract manufacturer in Elgin, Illinois. We build printed circuit board assemblies and finished electronic products for OEMs, from a single prototype to full-scale production. Our services include SMT, through-hole and mixed-technology PCB assembly, BGA assembly, box build and system integration, cable and wire harness assembly, conformal coating, potting, and functional testing. We offer turnkey, partial-turnkey and consignment models, so you can supply the parts, have us source them, or split the two. Every order starts with a DFM review to catch design issues before production. In-house inspection includes 3D AOI, X-ray for hidden BGA joints, and functional testing. Component lots are tracked through our ERP system, and records are stored in the cloud for audit-ready traceability. We support medical, aerospace, defense, industrial, transportation, LED and lighting, and consumer electronics customers. Our certifications include ISO 13485:2016, AS9100D and IPC-A-610 workmanship standards, and we are ITAR registered. We work in high-mix, low-to-mid volume programs and can build up to 1,000 PCBAs per day from our 20,000 sq. ft. facility. Prototypes typically ship in 5 to 7 business days.
