12a. Keyword Position And What “Permanent” Means

Positions within a Circuits-Keyword™ listing are ordered by when each listing was claimed. The company that claims a keyword first holds the first position, the next holds the second, and so on. Later applicants cannot buy their way past an earlier one.

Your position is held for as long as your subscription remains active. It is not a one-off purchase and it is not owned outright. If a subscription lapses, is cancelled, or fails to renew after any grace period, the listing stops being displayed and the position is released to the next company in order. Your company data is retained so the listing can be restored if you return, but the original position is not guaranteed to still be available.

“Beta pricing” means the rate agreed at sign-up is held for as long as the subscription stays continuously active. A lapse ends that rate.

12b. Trust Badges

Trust Badges such as “Authorized”, “Featured”, “Preferred”, “Specialist” and “Premium” are a paid presentation option. They describe how a company chooses to present itself and are not a statement by Circuits.com about that company. A badge may not use wording that implies Circuits.com has certified, verified, approved or endorsed the company.

The Circuits.com badge is different. It marks a listing run by the Circuits.com team itself. It cannot be purchased and cannot be self-assigned — only Circuits.com staff can apply it.

12c. What Circuits.com Checks

This section is what the short note at the foot of every company profile links to. In plain terms, here is the line between what a company tells us and what we have looked at ourselves.

Supplied by the company, and not checked by us: the company name, description, contact details, address, opening hours, staff list, images, documents, and any certifications or approvals it lists — including certificate files it uploads. A certification shown on a profile means the company says it holds it. It does not mean we have seen or confirmed it.

Chosen and paid for by the company: Trust Badges, and the ★ symbol marking an exclusive Circuits-Keyword™ sponsorship. Neither is a quality judgement by us.

Applied by Circuits.com: the Circuits.com badge, whether a listing is approved to appear at all, whether a listing has been claimed by its company, and whether a buyer review is published.

Companies warrant the accuracy of everything they supply under section 9, and we can remove a listing under section 16 when they do not. Buyers doing significant business should still confirm certifications and credentials with the company or the issuing body directly.
