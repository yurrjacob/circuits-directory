Powernexu

Server & Industrial Power Supply Solutions

Powernexu Technology Co Limited is a Hong Kong-based supplier specializing in server and industrial power supply solutions for data centers, AI computing infrastructure, enterprise IT systems, and industrial equipment. Our product portfolio includes CRPS redundant server power supplies, 12V and 54V server power supplies, industrial AC-DC power supplies, open-frame power supplies, and power distribution boards (PDBs). We supply power solutions from established manufacturers, including Great Wall, Huntkey, and Gospower, supporting customers with power supply sourcing, component selection, and application-specific requirements. Our focus is on helping customers identify suitable power solutions based on electrical specifications, mechanical compatibility, efficiency requirements, and system integration needs.
