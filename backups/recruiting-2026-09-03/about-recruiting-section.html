Recruiting on Circuits.com

The same Circuits-Keyword™ engine that connects buyers with suppliers also connects the people of the circuits industry with the companies that hire them.

Recruiting has two sides, and one free account can use both.

Seeking Employment. Engineers, technicians, recruiters, researchers and educators fill in their experience, choose up to 10 Circuits-Keywords™ that describe their skills, and appear under Seeking Employment for every one of them. Your title, experience and keywords are open to the industry; your name, contact details and resume stay private until an employer unlocks them. The Hiring side is free to search and free to apply to.

Hiring. Every account has a Hiring tab. Post open roles under the Circuits-Keywords™ you hire for; each post runs for 30 days under Hiring and applicants arrive with a link to their profile. Talent Access, a monthly subscription, unlocks the private details of anyone seeking employment so you can reach them directly.
