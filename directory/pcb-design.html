PCB Design Services

Schematic capture through to manufacture ready board layout.

PCB design covers schematic capture, component selection and footprint work, board layout, and the output package that a manufacturer needs to build from. The difficulty scales quickly with speed and density. A simple two layer board is routine work, while a multilayer design with controlled impedance, tight signal integrity requirements, or dense fine pitch packages calls for genuine specialism.

Design for manufacture is where a good layout firm earns its fee. Boards that route beautifully on screen can still be expensive or unreliable to assemble, and catching that before fabrication is far cheaper than after.

What buyers usually specify

  • Layer count and whether controlled impedance is required
  • Signal integrity, high speed, or RF requirements
  • Design tools used, and whether source files are handed over
  • Design for manufacture and design for test review
  • Output package: fabrication drawings, assembly drawings, and bill of materials
