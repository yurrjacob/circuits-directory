Electronics Buyers and Procurement

Sourcing and procurement professionals working across the electronics supply chain.

Procurement in electronics carries a heavier technical load than in most industries. Buyers work with allocation, long lead times, obsolescence, approved vendor lists, and the constant question of whether an alternate part is genuinely equivalent.

Listings here cover purchasing professionals, sourcing consultants, and supply chain specialists working within the circuits industry, along with firms offering procurement as a service.

What buyers usually specify

  • Categories covered: semiconductors, passives, electromechanical, or assemblies
  • Experience with allocation, shortage, and obsolescence management
  • Approved vendor list and qualification process familiarity
  • Regions and supply chains covered
  • Whether services are advisory or transactional
