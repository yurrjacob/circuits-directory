Electronics Manufacturers

Component makers and contract manufacturers, from prototype runs through to volume production.

Manufacturing in this industry covers two broad groups. Component manufacturers produce the parts themselves, from passives and connectors through to integrated circuits. Contract electronics manufacturers, often called CMs or EMS providers, assemble those parts into finished boards and products for other companies.

Selection usually comes down to volume fit. A firm geared for high volume production is rarely the right choice for a fifty unit prototype run, and the reverse is equally true. Certifications, traceability, and testing capability tend to decide the shortlist.

What buyers usually specify

  • Volume range the facility is set up for, from prototype to mass production
  • Assembly capability: surface mount, through hole, mixed, and fine pitch work
  • Quality certifications relevant to your sector
  • In house testing, inspection, and rework capability
  • Component sourcing: turnkey, consigned, or a mix
