Electronic Component Distributors

Authorised and independent distributors, franchise lines, and stocking suppliers.

Distribution splits into authorised and independent channels. Authorised distributors hold franchise agreements directly with manufacturers, which gives a clean traceability chain back to the factory. Independent distributors trade on the open market and can often locate obsolete, allocated, or end of life parts that the authorised channel no longer carries.

Both have a place. The authorised channel is the default for production sourcing. The independent channel becomes valuable during shortages and for legacy support, where verification and inspection practices matter a great deal more.

What buyers usually specify

  • Authorised franchise lines, or independent open market sourcing
  • Traceability documentation and certificates of conformance
  • Counterfeit avoidance: inspection, testing, and reporting practices
  • Stock position, lead times, and minimum order quantities
  • Support for obsolete and end of life parts
