Circuits Industry Engineers

Engineering talent across hardware, firmware, RF, and test, listed by the specialisms buyers actually search for.

Engineering work in the circuits industry spans a wide range of specialisms that rarely sit inside one company. A project may need schematic and board design, embedded firmware, RF and antenna work, power supply design, compliance and EMC testing, or production test development. Many teams source these individually as projects arise.

Listings under this Circuits-Keyword cover independent engineers, engineering consultancies, and design services firms who take on contract and project work for the electronics industry.

What buyers usually specify

  • Discipline: analogue, digital, RF, power, embedded firmware, or mixed signal
  • Whether the engagement is design only, or carried through to production support
  • Industry experience, especially where regulated sectors are involved
  • Ownership of design files and intellectual property at handover
  • Whether compliance and certification support is included
