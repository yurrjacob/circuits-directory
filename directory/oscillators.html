Oscillators and Frequency Control

Crystal oscillators and frequency control products, from general purpose to high stability.

An oscillator supplies the reference frequency a circuit is timed against. The plain crystal oscillator covers most general purpose work. Where stability across temperature matters, temperature compensated and oven controlled variants take over, at a corresponding increase in cost, size, and power draw.

Voltage controlled types allow the frequency to be tuned in circuit, which is what phase locked loop and clock recovery designs depend on. The choice is usually driven by how much frequency error the system can tolerate over its operating range.

What buyers usually specify

  • Frequency, and stability in parts per million across the temperature range
  • Type: XO, TCXO, VCXO, or OCXO
  • Output logic level and load requirements
  • Phase noise and jitter, where timing is critical
  • Package size, supply voltage, and power consumption
