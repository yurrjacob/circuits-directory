RF Transceivers

Transmit and receive in one device, across sub-GHz, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and cellular.

A transceiver combines transmitter and receiver in a single device, which is what most two way wireless links are built around. They arrive either as bare ICs, which demand RF design and regulatory work from the customer, or as pre-certified modules that carry much of that burden already.

The module route costs more per unit but removes a great deal of certification effort and schedule risk. For lower volumes that trade is usually worth making, and the calculation shifts as volume rises.

What buyers usually specify

  • Frequency band and the protocol or standard being targeted
  • Transmit power and receive sensitivity, which together set the link budget
  • IC or pre-certified module, and which regulatory approvals are carried
  • Supply voltage and current draw in transmit, receive, and sleep
  • Host interface, and whether a protocol stack is supplied
