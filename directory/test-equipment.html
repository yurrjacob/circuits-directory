Electronic Test Equipment

Bench and production test instruments, plus calibration and rental services.

Test equipment divides between development work and production work. A development bench prioritises flexibility, so oscilloscopes, meters, spectrum analysers, and programmable supplies dominate. Production test prioritises speed and repeatability, which tends toward fixtures and automated systems built around the specific product.

Calibration is the recurring commitment that is easy to overlook at purchase. Instruments used for any measurement that must be defensible need traceable calibration on a schedule, and that cost continues for the life of the instrument.

What buyers usually specify

  • Bandwidth, sample rate, and channel count for oscilloscopes
  • Frequency range and dynamic range for spectrum analysis
  • Accuracy and resolution for meters and sources
  • Calibration traceability and interval
  • Purchase, rental, or lease, and available service support
