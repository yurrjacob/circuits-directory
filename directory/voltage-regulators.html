Voltage Regulators

Linear and switching regulators, from low dropout devices to buck and boost converters.

Voltage regulators hold a supply rail steady as input voltage and load current vary. Linear regulators, including low dropout types, are quiet and simple but dissipate the difference between input and output as heat, which limits them where that gap or the current is large.

Switching regulators handle those cases far more efficiently by storing and releasing energy through an inductor, at the cost of switching noise and a more demanding layout. Designs sensitive to noise often use a switcher for the bulk conversion and a linear regulator afterwards to clean up the rail.

What buyers usually specify

  • Input voltage range and required output voltage, fixed or adjustable
  • Maximum load current, and thermal behaviour at that current
  • Dropout voltage for linear parts, efficiency for switching parts
  • Switching frequency and output noise where sensitive circuits follow
  • Protection: current limit, thermal shutdown, and soft start
