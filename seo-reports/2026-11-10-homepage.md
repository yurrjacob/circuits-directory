## Critical

None. Nothing on this page blocks indexing.

## High

### P1. The four Start cards and the account links all point into `/portal`, which robots.txt disallows
- **Observation:** the homepage's main content below the search is four cards linking to `/portal#listings`, `/portal#hiring`, `/portal#seeking` and `/portal#club` (`index.html:161-179`). Sign In, Dashboard and Admin also go to `/portal`. robots.txt has `Disallow: /portal`. The most prominent internal links on the strongest page lead to URLs Google is told not to crawl, so they pass nothing to pages that could rank.
- **Change:** point the cards at the public, indexable pages that explain each offer, and let those pages send people on to the portal:
  - Free Directory Listing → `/join` (indexable, but missing from the live sitemap)
  - Post Free Job → `/jobs`
  - Post Free Resume → `/talent`
  - Set Up a Student Club → `/clubs`

  If the cards must open the portal directly, keep them as they are and add a visible text link to each public page under each card.
- **Connects to:** audit M5 (internal links) and C1 (`/join`, `/clubs` and `/global` belong in the sitemap).
- **How we'd know it failed:** Search Console still shows `/join`, `/clubs` and `/global` as "Discovered, currently not indexed" 4 weeks after the change.
- **Leading indicator:** the internal-link counts for those pages in Search Console > Links.

### P2. Make the H1 visible and add real headings (audit H3)
- **Observation:** the only H1 is `sr-only`, and there are no H2s. The card titles are `<span class="wc-card-b">`, so the page has no visible outline for a crawler or a screen reader to follow.
- **Change:**
  - Show the H1 under the logo, shortened to something like "The free directory for electronics suppliers, parts and engineers".
  - Make the four card titles `<h2>` (keep the class, so the styling doesn't change).
- **How we'd know it failed:** a rendered snapshot still shows no visible H1, or the homepage's non-brand impressions stay flat 4 weeks after the change.
- **Leading indicator:** non-brand queries in Search Console > Performance, filtered to page `/`.

### P3. Add a short plain-language section with proof (audit H3)
- **Observation:** at 233 visible words, almost all UI labels, the page never says in buyer terms what the site is. "PCB assembly", "semiconductors" and "distributors" appear only in the meta tags and in hidden or link text.
- **Change:** add 250 to 350 words below the cards:
  - one paragraph on what Circuits.com is and who it serves
  - a three-step "how it works" (list, get found, get contacted)
  - live counts of companies, keywords, jobs and clubs, with an "as of" date
  - links to `/about`, `/global` and `/clubs`
- **How we'd know it failed:** word count is still under 500, or the "Popular" result pages remain the only places these terms appear.
- **Leading indicator:** impressions for "electronics supplier directory" and "<niche> suppliers" queries.

## Medium

- **P4. Retitle around a searchable phrase.** Suggested title: `Electronics Supplier Directory, Jobs & Talent | Circuits.com` (60 chars). Suggested description, 155 chars, with the jargon dropped: `Free directory of electronics suppliers, distributors, PCB assembly and semiconductor companies, plus an engineering job board and talent board. List free.` Update og:title, og:description and the Twitter tags to match.
- **P5. Search without JavaScript (audit M1).** `#home-form` has no `action` and the input has no `name`. Add `action="/results" method="get"` and `name="q"`. `initHome()` can still intercept the submit to route the other three tabs.
- **P6. CSS weight.** `styles.css` is 205 KB and blocks rendering on a page that uses a small part of it. Inline the homepage's above-the-fold rules, or split out a `home.css`. Re-run `node tools/stamp.js` afterwards. Also move the hero logo to SVG (preferred for a wordmark) or WebP.
- **P7. Three of the four "Popular" rows are `hidden`.** Their 20 links are still in the HTML and still crawlable, but hidden text carries less weight. This is fine as a UI choice. Just don't count on those rows for relevance; that is P3's job.

## Low

- **P8. `og:image:alt`, `og:image:width` and `og:image:height`** are missing. Add `og:image:alt` with "Circuits.com, the circuits industry directory", plus 1200 and 630.
- **P9. Favicon.** It is a 64x64 PNG at a relative path. Add a 48px multiple (for example 192x192) and an `apple-touch-icon`.
- **P10. Generic anchors.** "Engineers", "Buyers" and "Manufacturers" under Popular are vague. "Electronics Engineers" and "Electronics Manufacturers" say more.
- **P11. Source comments.** The comments at `index.html:69-73`, `82-83`, `88-93`, `144-145` and `156-158` include a staff first name and dates. Visitors don't see them, but they are in the public source (audit Low).
- **Info:** Google retired the sitelinks search box in November 2024, so the `SearchAction` no longer produces a SERP feature. It is harmless and other engines can still read it, so leave it in.

## Passing

- A single, self-referencing canonical, no robots restrictions, and HTTPS with clean 301s.
- Complete OG and Twitter tags with a correctly sized 1200x630 card.
- Every image has alt text and explicit dimensions, and the hero has `fetchpriority="high"`. The notifications bell ships in the markup to avoid layout shift.
- The search tabs use real buttons with `role="tab"` and `aria-selected`, and the input is labelled.
- No em dashes in visible text.
