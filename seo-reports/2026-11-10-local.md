# Local SEO analysis: circuits.com

Date: 2026-11-10. Sources: live fetch of https://circuits.com (HTTP 200) and the repository source (`index.html`, `contact.html`, `profile.js`, `sitemap.xml`, `store.js`).

## Verdict: local SEO mostly does not apply

Circuits.com is a national B2B industry directory, with a job board and a talent board. It is not a local business. The homepage has:

- no street address, opening hours, service area or Maps embed
- no LocalBusiness schema (only `Organization` and `WebSite`)
- no Google Business Profile signals
- a "city + service" title or H1 would be the wrong goal: the site targets "circuits industry directory", not a place

So a 0 to 100 Local SEO Score would just record that this isn't a local business, and it would be misleading. I scored only the parts that apply.

| Dimension | Applies? | Status |
|---|---|---|
| GBP signals (25%) | No. There's no storefront, and a GBP for an online directory risks suspension under Google's eligibility rules | n/a |
| Reviews (20%) | Only for listed companies. Buyer reviews are switched off (`profile.js:451`) | n/a for now |
| Local on-page (20%) | Partly: location data on listings | Weak |
| NAP and citations (15%) | Yes, for Circuits.com's own entity | Weak |
| Local schema (10%) | Yes, for listing pages | Weak |
| Local authority (10%) | Yes, as industry and brand authority, not local | See the full audit |

## Where local signals actually matter

### 1. Circuits.com's own entity (NAP)

| Source | Name | Address | Phone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Visible HTML (`/contact`) | Circuits.com, plus two named founders | none | two 631 (Long Island, NY) numbers as `tel:` links |
| Organization JSON-LD (`index.html`) | Circuits.com | none | none |
| GBP | none found | none | none |

There are no outright conflicts, but the entity is thin. The schema has no `telephone`, `address`, `contactPoint` or `sameAs`, and no page names a legal entity or a mailing address. This matches finding 80 of the full audit.

### 2. Listing pages: location data is invisible to crawlers

Each company profile has a free-text `address`, and jobs have a `location`. Both are drawn only in the browser:

- `profile.js:198, 488, 628` render the address client-side.
- `profile.js:706-723` inject Organization JSON-LD at runtime, with `address` as a **plain string** instead of a `PostalAddress`. That gives Google no machine-readable city, region or country.
- The sitemap lists 8 static pages and no company profiles, so profiles can't be found by location either.

Searches like "PCB assembly Massachusetts" or "electronics contract manufacturer near me" are where a directory picks up local-intent traffic. Right now it can't compete for them.

### 3. Reviews

Buyer reviews are off, and `reviews` is a hardcoded empty array (`profile.js:453`), so no `aggregateRating` is emitted. That's correct. If reviews come back, the markup is allowed because Circuits.com is a third party reviewing other businesses. Emit it only when the reviews are visible on that same page.

## Prioritized actions

1. **High.** Server-render listing location. Extend `tools/build-profiles.js` (already planned in the full audit, finding 44) so each profile page carries the address in its HTML and JSON-LD, with `address` as a `PostalAddress` (`addressLocality`, `addressRegion`, `addressCountry`). This needs structured columns on `companies`, or parsing of the free-text field. Any new column needs an anon SELECT grant (see `tools/rls-check.sql`).
   - *Failure check:* Google's Rich Results Test on a built profile shows no `PostalAddress`.
   - *Leading indicator:* Search Console impressions for queries containing a US state or city name.
2. **High.** Add profile URLs to `sitemap.xml` once they are server-rendered. This depends on action 1.
3. **Medium.** Strengthen the Organization entity on the homepage: add `contactPoint` (telephone, `contactType: "customer support"`, `areaServed: "US"`), `sameAs` (LinkedIn and other official profiles), and a postal address or legal name if you're willing to publish one.
   - *Failure check:* a branded search doesn't produce a knowledge panel within a few months.
4. **Medium.** Claim **Bing Places** and an **Apple Business** listing only if you have a qualifying address. Otherwise, put the effort into the citations AI assistants draw on for B2B: LinkedIn company page, Crunchbase, and industry associations such as IPC and SMTA.
5. **Low, and only with care.** Region landing pages (for example `/pcb-assembly/massachusetts`) could capture local-intent searches. They are programmatic pages: build one only where at least about 5 real listings exist. Stop at 30 pages unless each has at least 60% unique content, and require explicit sign-off before going past 50. Otherwise they read as doorway pages.
6. **Do not** create a Google Business Profile for Circuits.com unless you have staffed premises that customers visit. An online-only directory isn't eligible.

## Limitations

This analysis could not check geo-grid rankings, local pack positions, GBP Insights, Domain Authority or full backlinks. Those need DataForSEO, BrightLocal or Whitespark, plus Search Console access. No GBP listing was supplied or found.
