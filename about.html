About Circuits.com

Circuits.com is the world’s intelligent search index for the entire circuits industry. Our mission is simple: connect the people, products, and technologies that power the industry through one central platform.

The Marketplace: Search, List & Connect

Whether you are a buyer sourcing components, a company offering services, or an independent professional, Circuits.com eliminates complicated categories. Just search or get listed to connect instantly.

  • For Buyers: Search any part number, manufacturer, or technology to find relevant suppliers and engineers. No accounts required, just search.
  • For Companies & Professionals: Establish your presence where the industry searches. Secure a listing under the specific keywords that best represent your products, design services, or technical expertise.
  • The Circuits-Keyword™ Model: Listings rotate randomly to guarantee equal organic visibility for every participating business.
  • Premium Visibility: Companies can upgrade to secure exclusive sponsor banners, fixed top placements, and credibility trust badges.

Recruiting on Circuits.com

Our proprietary keyword engine also connects industry talent with hiring companies. This dual-sided recruitment platform is 100% free for both parties.

  • Post a Free Resume: Engineers, technicians, educators and freshers can build a profile and choose up to 10 industry keywords. You will automatically appear on the Recruit Board when hiring managers search those terms.
  • Post a Free Job: Companies can post open roles under targeted keywords. Approved posts run for 30 days on the Job Board, allowing you to view applicant profiles and contact details at no cost.
