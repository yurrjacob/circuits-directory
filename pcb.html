Huayihai

Simplifying Complex Builds with Expert Support

**Huayihai, PCB & PCBA Manufacturing Services** Founded in 2005, Shenzhen Huayihai Technology Co., Ltd. is a PCB and PCBA manufacturing company based in Shenzhen, China. With over 20 years of industry experience, an 8,000 m² manufacturing facility, and more than 300 employees, Huayihai provides PCB fabrication, electronic component sourcing, and PCB assembly services to customers worldwide. Our capabilities cover rigid PCB, multilayer PCB, HDI PCB, flexible PCB, aluminum PCB, and high-frequency PCB manufacturing, together with SMT and DIP assembly, BGA assembly, and quality inspection. We support projects from prototype production to volume manufacturing based on customers' Gerber files, BOMs, and technical specifications. Huayihai serves customers in industrial electronics, automotive electronics, medical electronics, communication equipment, and other electronic applications, with a focus on reliable manufacturing, consistent quality, and responsive service.
